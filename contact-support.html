Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I create an account?

Download the app from the Apple App Store, open it, and follow the registration instructions. You will need a valid email address.

2. I forgot my password. What should I do?

Select “Forgot Password” on the login screen and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

3. Is my journal data private?

Yes. Your personal data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and stored securely. Please review our Privacy Policy page for full details.

4. The app is not working properly.

Please ensure:

  • Your device is updated to the latest iOS version.
  • You are using the latest version of LCI Wellbeing Journal.
  • You have a stable internet connection.

If the issue persists, contact us via email with details of the problem and your device model.

Technical Requirements
